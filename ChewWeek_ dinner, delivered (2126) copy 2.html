Questions

How does ChewWeek work?

You choose menus for the week. Each pack holds one gum with three flavors. The app counts your chews and moves you from soup to main to dessert.

What is the blueberry level?

Dessert is powerful. The meter fills as you chew, and the STOP alert tells you when to spit. Past 100%, you turn blue.

Can I skip a week?

Yes. Tap a day in your box and remove it. Dinner is still a ritual, so we won’t guilt you.

Is this a real product?

No. It’s a speculative prototype from the IP1A course “Speculative Gastronomy.” It asks what happens to dinner when time and farmland run out.
